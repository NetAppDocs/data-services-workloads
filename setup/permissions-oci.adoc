= Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Storage permissions for NetApp Workload Factory
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ./media/

[.lead]
Review the permissions required for managing Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Storage resources with NetApp Workload Factory.

Cross-tenancy trust

NetApp operates the `workload-factory-OCI-SN` group in a NetApp-owned OCI tenancy, with API-key credentials mounted into a K8s secret. Each customer establishes a cross-tenancy policy (paired Endorse on the NetApp side, Admit on the customer side) that grants this group a narrow, explicit set of permissions in the customer's tenancy. Every OCI API call is evaluated against this contract per request; the customer can audit and revoke it at any time.

NetApp side

Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to read compartments in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to read instance-family in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to read virtual-network-family in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to read file-family in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to manage file-family in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to read metrics in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to inspect file-systems in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to manage functions-family in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to use virtual-network-family in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to read log-content in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to inspect log-groups in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to manage log-groups in any-tenancy
Endorse group workload-factory-OCI-SN to inspect tenancies in any-tenancy

Customer side (default — tenancy root)

Define tenancy NetAppWF as ocid1.tenancy.oc1..<>
Define group NetAppOpsWF as ocid1.group.oc1..<>
Admit group NetAppOpsWF of tenancy NetAppWF to inspect tenancies in tenancy
Admit group NetAppOpsWF of tenancy NetAppWF to read compartments in tenancy
Admit group NetAppOpsWF of tenancy NetAppWF to read instance-family in tenancy
Admit group NetAppOpsWF of tenancy NetAppWF to read metrics in tenancy
Admit group NetAppOpsWF of tenancy NetAppWF to read log-content in tenancy
Admit group NetAppOpsWF of tenancy NetAppWF to use virtual-network-family in tenancy
Admit group NetAppOpsWF of tenancy NetAppWF to manage file-family in tenancy
Admit group NetAppOpsWF of tenancy NetAppWF to manage functions-family in tenancy
Admit group NetAppOpsWF of tenancy NetAppWF to manage log-groups in tenancy

Optional: Customers who require least privilege can replace in tenancy with in compartment <name> on each line.
